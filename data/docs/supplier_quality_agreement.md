# QA-SUP-002 — Supplier Quality and Delivery Agreement

## 1. Delivery Performance

Suppliers must achieve an **on-time delivery rate of at least 95%**, measured on a rolling
basis as the share of received purchase orders whose received date is on or before the
promised date. Orders not yet received are excluded from the calculation.

## 2. Quality Performance

Incoming parts must meet a defect rate below 500 PPM (parts per million). Any shipment
causing a line stoppage triggers an 8D corrective action request within 48 hours.

## 3. Escalation Ladder

1. **Below 95% on-time for one quarter** — Supplier receives a written warning and must
   submit a recovery plan within 15 business days.
2. **Below 90% on-time, or two consecutive quarters below 95%** — Supplier is placed on
   probation. New orders require Supply Chain Director approval and dual sourcing must be
   evaluated.
3. **Below 85% on-time while on probation** — Supplier is disqualified for new parts and a
   transition plan to an alternate source is started.

## 4. Review Cadence

Supplier scorecards are reviewed quarterly by Supply Chain and the plant managers.
