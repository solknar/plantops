# SOP-LOTO-001 — Lockout/Tagout

## Purpose

Protect workers from the unexpected release of hazardous energy (electrical, hydraulic,
pneumatic, mechanical, thermal, gravitational) during service and maintenance. This SOP
implements OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 at all Northwind Fabrication plants.

## Scope

Applies to every machine in Detroit, Houston and Austin plants, including HX-500 presses,
CNC-7 machining centers, RW-3 welding cells and CV-200 conveyors.

## The Seven Steps of Lockout/Tagout

1. **Prepare** — Identify every energy source using the machine's energy control placard.
2. **Notify** — Tell affected operators and the shift supervisor that the machine will be locked out.
3. **Shut down** — Stop the machine using normal operating controls.
4. **Isolate** — Open disconnects and close valves for every energy source.
5. **Lock and tag** — Each authorized worker applies their own personal lock and tag. Never share locks.
6. **Release stored energy** — Bleed hydraulic accumulators and pneumatic lines to zero, block raised rams, discharge capacitors.
7. **Verify** — Attempt to start the machine and test with a meter or gauge to confirm zero energy.

## Removal of Locks

Only the worker who applied a lock may remove it. If that worker is unavailable, the plant
EHS manager may authorize removal after confirming the worker is off-site and notifying them.

## Training

Authorized employees are retrained annually and whenever a periodic inspection finds a deviation.
Violations of this SOP are a terminable safety offense.
