# POL-MRO-004 — Spare Parts and MRO Purchasing Policy

## 1. Critical Spares

The following parts are designated **critical spares** because their failure stops a whole
production line and supplier lead time exceeds one week:

- P-1001 HX-500 Seal Kit
- P-1002 HX-500 Hydraulic Pump
- P-2001 CNC-7 Spindle Bearing Set
- P-4002 CV-200 Gear Motor

Each plant must keep on-hand stock of every critical spare at or above its reorder point at
all times. When on-hand stock of a critical spare falls below the reorder point, the
maintenance planner must raise an expedited purchase order the same business day.

## 2. Reorder Rules for Non-Critical Parts

Non-critical parts are reordered in the weekly MRO run when on-hand stock is below the
reorder point stored in the inventory system.

## 3. Purchase Approval Limits

| Purchase order total | Required approval |
|----------------------|-------------------|
| Up to $5,000 | Maintenance planner |
| $5,000.01 to $25,000 | Plant manager |
| Above $25,000 | VP of Operations |

Expedited critical-spare orders may be placed before approval, but approval must be obtained
within two business days.

## 4. Preferred Suppliers

Orders must go to the preferred supplier listed for the part unless that supplier is on
probation under the Supplier Quality Agreement (QA-SUP-002).
