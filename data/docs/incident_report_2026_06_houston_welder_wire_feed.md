# RCA-2026-022 — W-05 Wire Feed Jams on Houston RW-3 Cells

**Date of report:** 2026-06-19
**Plant:** Houston Assembly (HOU)
**Equipment:** RW-3 cells HOU-L1-M4 and HOU-L2-M4

## Summary

Houston RW-3 welding cells logged recurring W-05 wire feed jams in Q2 2026. Chassis-frame
weld quality also dropped, with weld porosity becoming the most frequent defect type in
quality inspections.

## Root Cause

A switch to a new wire spool brand in April 2026 came with a stiffer spool brake. Brake
tension was set at roughly 4 N·m instead of the 2.5 N·m specified in the RW-3 maintenance
guide, which overloaded the feed rollers (P-3001) and wore them out within weeks.

## Corrective Actions

1. Reset spool brake tension to 2.5 N·m on all Houston cells and add the check to the weekly PM.
2. Replace feed roller kits on both cells (P-3001).
3. Increase Houston stock of P-3001 and contact tips P-3002; both ran out during the incident.
4. Ask WeldTech Supply (S-03) for an 8D on late roller kit deliveries.

## Open Risks

WeldTech Supply delivery performance is below the 95% on-time requirement of QA-SUP-002,
which delayed roller kit replenishment during the incident.
