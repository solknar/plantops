# CV-200 Powered Belt Conveyor — Service Notes

## Fault Codes

| Code | Meaning | Likely cause | First corrective step |
|------|---------|--------------|-----------------------|
| C-03 | Belt slip detected by speed sensor | Glazed or stretched drive belt (part P-4001), low tension | Re-tension belt to 1.5% elongation; replace if glazed |
| C-09 | Motor overload trip | Failing gear motor (part P-4002), jammed rollers, overloaded belt | Clear jams, check motor current vs nameplate 7.2 A; replace gear motor if current exceeds nameplate by more than 15% unloaded |

## Preventive Maintenance

- Weekly: check belt tracking and tension.
- Monthly: grease roller bearings; measure motor current.
- Annually: replace drive belt P-4001.
