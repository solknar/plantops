# RW-3 Robotic MIG Welding Cell — Maintenance Guide

## 1. Overview

The RW-3 is a six-axis robot with a MIG torch, wire feeder and spool. Wire: ER70S-6, 1.2 mm.

## 2. Fault Codes

| Code | Meaning | Likely cause | First corrective step |
|------|---------|--------------|-----------------------|
| W-05 | Wire feed jam | Worn feed rollers (part P-3001), kinked liner, spool brake tension too high | Check spool brake tension (target 2.5 N·m), then inspect rollers and liner |
| W-12 | Arc instability | Worn contact tip (part P-3002), spatter buildup, poor ground | Replace contact tip and clean nozzle |
| W-20 | Collision detect | Fixture crash, damaged torch cable (part P-3003) | Inspect torch and cable; re-run TCP (tool center point) calibration after any replacement |

Contact tips are consumables: replace every 8 operating hours or at any W-12 alarm.

## 3. Preventive Maintenance

| Interval | Task |
|----------|------|
| Every shift | Replace contact tip; ream nozzle |
| Weekly | Blow out liner; check spool brake tension |
| Every 3 months | Replace feed roller kit P-3001 and liner |
| After any W-20 | TCP calibration, verify within 0.5 mm |
